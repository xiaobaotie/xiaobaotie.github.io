Lantern Yard - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-06. Developer: ListeningStudio. Contact: 911164435@qq.com.

Lantern Yard ("the game") is a single-player offline game. This policy explains what data the game and its advertising partner handle.

Data the developer collects

None. The game has no account system, no sign-in, no chat and no analytics of its own. The developer does not receive, store or sell any personal information.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (levels cleared, stars, upgrades, sound settings) is saved only on your device. It is never uploaded. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Most ads are optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch; occasionally a full-screen ad may appear between levels. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

DataPurpose
Device identifiers, including the Android advertising IDAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Approximate location (from IP address)Advertising, fraud prevention
App interactions with ads (views, taps), app and device information, crash and performance data of the ad SDKAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention

This data is processed by Google under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Data is encrypted in transit.

Your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, and in other regions where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the "Ad Choices" link on the title screen.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • You can play the whole game without watching any rewarded ads.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and is not designed for them. Its theme is zombie survival with mild cartoon violence. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
