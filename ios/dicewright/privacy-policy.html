Dicewright - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-28. Developer: ListeningStudio. Contact: 911164435@qq.com.

Dicewright ("the game") is a single-player offline game. This policy explains what data the game and its partners handle.

Data the developer collects

None. The game has no account system, no sign-in, no chat and no analytics of its own. The developer does not receive, store or sell any personal information.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (run progress, unlocks, daily challenge records and settings) is saved only on your device. It is never uploaded. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes it.

Advertising and third parties

The game shows no ads and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. It does not use your device's advertising identifier and never asks for tracking permission. It does not connect to the internet.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and is not designed for them. It is a single-player dice strategy game with no violence and no gambling. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
